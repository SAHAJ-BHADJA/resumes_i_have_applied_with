Executive summary — board briefing

Between 07-19 09:14 and 07-21 01:41 UTC, an attacker breached Northwind Health by spear-phishing user j.reyes on WKS-2471, then stole cached credentials and pivoted to SRV-FILES01, escalating to domain-admin control of the domain controller (DC01). Protected health information was archived from the PHI file share and patient records were copied out of the northwind-phi-records-prod S3 bucket to attacker-controlled cloud storage — data exfiltration of PHI is CONFIRMED by cross-account object copies from an external IP. The intrusion spans 4 hosts and 3 accounts across 40.5 hours; 13 findings are CONFIRMED by multi-source evidence, while 1 remains UNCERTAIN due to log-coverage gaps (notably a database server without endpoint monitoring and partial cloud size logging).
